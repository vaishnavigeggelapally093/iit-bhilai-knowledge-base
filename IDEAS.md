# IDEAS - what the knowledge base can and cannot answer

## Questions it can support

1. **"Can I take CS252 next semester, and what must I finish first?"** The prerequisite graph and rule R016 can support an answer, subject to the legacy-scheme scope and checking the exact rule wording.
2. **"I'm weak in linear algebra - what can I study before Machine Learning (CS550)?"** Skill/resource mappings can suggest MA605/DS250/DS251 topics and linked resources. Three prerequisite semester-order checks remain unresolved because the source does not state the elective semester.
3. **"How many credits are required?"** The curriculum slots and rules represent 30 credits per semester and 240 total in the legacy scheme.

## Questions it cannot answer reliably

1. **"What courses should a current-scheme student take?"** The current-scheme CSE curriculum is not included; the course list is legacy.
2. **"Which elective will be offered in Semester 6, and who teaches it?"** The source does not specify elective offerings, and there is no instructor or timetable data.
3. **"Am I eligible for a minor or a particular NPTEL credit?"** No minor eligibility policy or NPTEL resource entries are included.
4. **"How ready am I personally for a course?"** There are nine optional author-created practice exercises, but no learner profile, attempt history, or progress data to estimate an individual's readiness.

## Useful next improvements

Add the current-scheme CSE curriculum from an authoritative source; capture exact PDF URLs and include the original Courses of Study PDF; independently verify prerequisites and rule wording; manually confirm resource licences; add explicit source-backed learning outcomes if available; and expand optional exercises only after the core data is verified.
